# Embody licensing FAQ

This FAQ explains the project's current understanding of the Elastic License 2.0 (ELv2). It is not legal advice, does not modify the license, and does not grant additional rights. The [LICENSE](../../LICENSE) text controls. If this FAQ and ELv2 differ, follow ELv2 and obtain your own legal advice.

## Is Embody open source?

No. Embody is **source-available** under ELv2, which is not an OSI-approved open-source license.

## What does ELv2 allow?

Subject to its conditions and limitations, ELv2 permits use, copying, distribution, making the software available, and preparing derivative works. It also contains a patent grant. Read the complete license before relying on those permissions.

## What are ELv2's principal limitations?

ELv2 says that you may not:

1. provide the software to third parties as a hosted or managed service where the service gives users access to any substantial set of the software's features or functionality;
2. move, change, disable, or circumvent license-key functionality, or remove or obscure functionality protected by a license key; or
3. alter, remove, or obscure licensing, copyright, or other notices.

Embody does not add limitations to ELv2 through this FAQ.

## May I use Embody internally?

ELv2 does not prohibit an individual or organization from running Embody for its own internal operations. Contractors and affiliates should independently assess who is receiving access and whether a proposed arrangement becomes a service to a third party.

## May I build and host an application using Embody?

The relevant ELv2 question is whether your service provides third parties access to a substantial set of **Embody's** features or functionality. An application whose users consume distinct domain functionality is different from offering Embody itself as a general-purpose managed platform. The boundary depends on the actual product and user access; this FAQ cannot decide a specific deployment for you.

## May a consultant deploy Embody for a customer?

ELv2 does not categorically prohibit paid consulting, deployment, training, or maintenance. A consultant must still assess whether the arrangement provides Embody to a third party as the prohibited kind of hosted or managed service. Customer-specific work is not automatically exempt from the license.

## May I redistribute Embody or a modified version?

ELv2 grants distribution rights subject to all its terms. Recipients must receive the applicable terms, and required licensing, copyright, and other notices must remain intact. Modifications do not become an official Embody release merely because redistribution is permitted.

## May I publish a proprietary plugin or application?

Embody does not claim ownership of independently authored applications or plugins merely because they use its public APIs. Your work may still be a derivative work depending on how it is created and distributed, and dependencies may have separate terms. Obtain legal advice for your circumstances.

Applications generated by `create-embody-app` are marked `UNLICENSED` by default. That means the generator does not choose a license for the application; it does not prevent the application's owner from selecting one.

## What does “substantial set of features or functionality” mean?

ELv2 does not provide a numeric threshold. Relevant facts may include which Embody capabilities users can access, whether the service substitutes for obtaining and operating Embody, and whether Embody itself—rather than a distinct application—is the service being offered. Only qualified counsel can evaluate a particular offering.

## Can I use Embody's name or logo?

The software license does not grant trademark rights. Accurate, non-misleading references such as “built with Embody” may be permitted by applicable law, but you must not imply sponsorship, certification, partnership, or official status. Do not use Embody branding in a product or domain name in a way likely to cause confusion. A separate counsel-approved trademark policy may supersede this interim guidance.

## Are commercial exceptions available?

The rightsholder may enter into a separate written agreement granting rights outside ELv2. No commercial offering, pricing, support level, exception, or permission exists unless confirmed in a written agreement signed by an authorized party. Open an issue containing no confidential information to request an appropriate private contact channel.

## Where should licensing questions go?

For general, non-confidential questions, open a GitHub issue labeled `licensing`. Do not rely on issue comments as a binding exception. For a binding interpretation or rights outside ELv2, obtain a written agreement from the rightsholder and consult your own lawyer.
